About YJ Technosoft
Private Limited

YJ Technosoft Private Limited is a technology and software development company focused on building digital solutions that help organizations improve operational efficiency, streamline workflows and modernize business processes.

Incorporated in 2021 and headquartered in Kawardha, Chhattisgarh, the company's project portfolio spans government digitization, enterprise application development, healthcare technology, business communication platforms, legal case management, real estate CRM, procurement support, human resources and payroll systems, and digital marketplaces.

YJ Technosoft approaches software development with an emphasis on understanding operational requirements, designing practical technology solutions, developing user-oriented applications and supporting digital implementation. Its capabilities encompass web and mobile application development, backend systems, API integration, database design, workflow automation and application deployment.

The company works with government institutions, enterprises, service providers and growing businesses to translate operational requirements into reliable, maintainable software solutions.

Core focus: practical technology, process digitization and custom software solutions aligned with organizational needs.

03 · Government Digital Transformation

Digitizing Public Service
Workflows

Government offices run on registers, files and follow-ups. YJ Technosoft builds systems that move those records into secure, searchable digital workflows, without forcing staff to change how the work itself is done.

Our approach for public institutions

  • Understand the register first. Each system starts from the physical registers, forms and reporting formats already in use.
  • Role-based access. Officers, operators and supervisors see only what their role permits.
  • Audit trails. Entries and changes are recorded so records remain accountable.
  • Search and retrieval. Archived records are retrievable by number, date, name or category in place of manual file search.
  • Reports in familiar formats. Output matches the formats officers already submit.
  • On-site training and support. Staff are trained on the system and supported after go-live.

Typical modules

Complaint registration

Digital intake, numbering, status tracking and disposal.

Digital registers

Station / office registers maintained electronically.

Archiving

Secure storage and retrieval of historical records.

Departmental MIS

Dashboards and periodic reports for supervisors.

Case & court tracking

Case status, hearing dates and follow-ups.

Alerts

SMS / email notifications for pending actions.

CGP Digital — Raigarh Police

ClientOffice of the Superintendent of Police, Raigarh, Chhattisgarh
SectorGovernment · Law enforcement
SolutionDigital complaint registration, archiving and police-station register management
YJ Technosoft's roleDevelopment / implementation / deployment / maintenance (confirm)
Delivery yearYear
UsersPolice stations / offices / user roles covered
StatusCompleted and delivered

The challenge

Police stations maintain complaints and a range of statutory and administrative registers on paper. Paper records are slow to search, difficult to consolidate for supervisory review and vulnerable to wear and loss over time.

The solution

  • Complaint registration: structured digital entry of complaints with unique numbering and status tracking.
  • Register management: police-station registers maintained in digital form.
  • Digital archive: historical records stored securely and retrievable through search.
  • Role-based access: separate permissions for station staff and supervising officers.
  • Reports: summaries for supervisory review. confirm report types

Systems built for public institutions and businesses hold sensitive records. Security controls are part of the design, not an add-on.

Access control

  • Individual user logins
  • Role-based permissions
  • Hashed passwords; token-based sessions

Data protection

  • HTTPS / SSL on all deployments
  • Scheduled database backups
  • Private storage for uploaded documents

Accountability

  • Audit trails on record changes
  • Message and activity logs

Confidentiality

  • NDA available for every engagement
  • Client data never used in marketing
  • Redacted samples only in demonstrations
