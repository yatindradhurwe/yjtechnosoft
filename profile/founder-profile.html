Profile

Technology entrepreneur and founder of YJ Technosoft Private Limited, a software development company incorporated in 2021 in Kawardha, Chhattisgarh.

Leads the company's work in government digitization and enterprise software, from requirement study with clients through design, development, deployment and long-term support. Under this leadership the company has delivered platforms across policing, healthcare, legal, real estate, procurement, HR and payroll, communications and consumer services, including CGP Digital for Raigarh Police.

Focused on practical technology: software that matches how an office or business actually works, is easy for staff to adopt, and is supported after it goes live.
